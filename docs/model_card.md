## 2. Model Architecture

The intended Pannaga production forecasting engine integrates global teleconnection indices with spatial numerical weather prediction (NWP) signals:

1. **XGBoost Three-Head Ensemble (`app.ml.models.XGBoostForecaster`):**
   - Head A: `onset_model` — Probability of operational monsoon onset within the 7-day target window.
   - Head B: `break_model` — Probability of an active monsoon break (dry spell of $\ge 5$ consecutive days with $<2.5\text{ mm/day}$).
   - Head C: `excess_model` — Probability of extreme downpours exceeding the 80th climatological percentile.
2. **Spatial ConvLSTM Head (`app.ml.models.ConvLSTMForecaster`):**
   - 2-layer recurrent convolutional network (64 hidden dimensions, $3\times3$ spatial kernels).
   - Designed to propagate spatial coherence and synoptic-scale monsoon trough migrations across contiguous Blocks and Gram Panchayats.
3. **Deterministic Physical Prior Simulator (`app.ml.simulator`):**
   - Active fallback engine in Phase 3.
   - Computes sign-correct climatological baselines adjusted by real-time teleconnections:
     - **ENSO (Nino 3.4 / ONI):** El Niño suppresses onset probability and increases break risk; La Niña accelerates onset and enhances rainfall.
     - **IOD (Dipole Mode Index):** Positive Indian Ocean Dipole suppresses monsoon rainfall; Negative IOD enhances moisture convergence.
     - **MJO (Madden-Julian Oscillation RMM):** Phases 2–3 (over the Indian Ocean) enhance onset; Phases 6–7 (Western Pacific) induce dry spells.
     - **Latitudinal Gradient:** Captures northward propagation from Kerala ($8^\circ\text{N}$) to Punjab ($31^\circ\text{N}$).

## 4. Evaluation Protocol for Future Training (Phase 8+)

When empirical training is executed against the multi-decadal CHIRPS / IMD hindcast array, evaluation MUST adhere to the following protocol:

- **Cross-Validation Scheme:** Leave-One-Year-Out (LOYO) cross-validation across all seasons from 1981 to 2023. Training on year $Y$ must strictly exclude observations from year $Y$.
- **Baseline Benchmarks:** Every model head must be scored against both:
  1. Climatological base rate (sample frequency per pentad/week).
  2. 1-week persistence baseline.
- **Reporting Metrics:**
  - **Brier Skill Score (BSS):** $BSS = 1 - \frac{BS_{\text{model}}}{BS_{\text{climatology}}}$. A positive score demonstrates skill superior to climatology.
  - **Area Under ROC Curve (AUC-ROC):** Assessed independently across Lead Weeks 1, 2, 3, and 4.
  - **Reliability Diagrams & ECE:** Expected Calibration Error must be reported to verify probability honesty.
  - **Rainfall MAE / CRPS:** Continuous Ranked Probability Score on quantile precipitation predictions ($P_{10}, P_{50}, P_{90}$).

## 5. Ethical Considerations & Safety Boundaries

1. **Agronomic Risk Mitigation:** Probabilities are never converted to binary decisions by the machine learning system alone. A human-in-the-loop review by Agricultural Extension Officers is mandated for `HIGH` and `CRITICAL` alerts.
2. **Failure Fallback:** In the event of NWP or index ingestion outage, the engine gracefully falls back to deterministic climatology, tagging all outputs as `SIMULATED`.
3. **No Phantom Precision:** Predictions are explicitly presented at 7-day aggregated horizons. Daily downscaled precision is prohibited beyond 5 days.
